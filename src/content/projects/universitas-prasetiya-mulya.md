Our Collaboration with Universitas Prasetiya Mulya. We’ve transform 210kg plastic waste mixed from plastic bottle cap and shampoo bottle to create several product, such as Garden Bench, Garden Fence, & Signage Board.

## Solution to Plastic Waste : Reduce, Recycle, Upcycle

At the heart of this endeavor lies a simple yet powerful message: reduce, recycle, and upcycle. By reducing the consumption of single-use plastics and repurposing them into new, valuable items, the project aims to inspire a shift towards more sustainable practices.

In the bustling grounds of Universitas Prasetiya Mulya, a unique initiative is taking root, demonstrating the power of creativity and environmental consciousness. The project, born from a desire to promote sustainability and combat plastic waste, involves the transformation of 210kg of plastic bottles and shampoo bottle caps into functional and beautiful additions to the campus garden, called “Pocket Forest”.

## The Transformation Process

The journey begins with the collection of plastic bottles and shampoo bottle caps from various sources within the local community, Bank Sampah, and stakeholders from campus.

Next comes the recycling phase, where the collected plastic waste is carefully sorted and processed. Through innovative techniques and dedication, the plastic is transformed into raw materials suitable for upcycling. Here are how it turn out, one of the benches that have been used in “Pocket Forest” comes from waste.

With creativity and craftsmanship, the recycled plastic materials are molded and shaped into benches and fences for the Pocket Forest. Skilled hands bring forth beauty from what was once considered waste, turning it into functional pieces that serve both practical and aesthetic purposes.
